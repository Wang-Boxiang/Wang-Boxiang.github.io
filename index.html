I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore. My research focuses on deep learning-based active noise control (ANC) under dynamic acoustic conditions: noise that moves, changes its spectrum, and reaches the listener through acoustic paths that vary with the room, the device and the wearer.

My research interests include:

  • Active Noise Control and Adaptive Filtering — feedforward and feedback ANC, deep learning-based control filter design, adaptive filtering under time-varying acoustic paths, virtual sensing, and multichannel ANC for rooms and aircraft cabins.
  • Spatial Audio and Microphone Array Processing — sound source localization, direction-of-arrival estimation, moving-source tracking in reverberant rooms, and spatially selective noise control.
  • Efficient On-Device Audio AI — lightweight neural networks, low-latency real-time processing, and embedded implementation on headphones and audio chips.
