I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. My research focuses on deep learning-based active noise control and spatial audio for hearables and other audio devices.

My research interests include:

  • Deep Learning for Audio Front-End Processing — combining deep learning with adaptive filtering for active noise control that adapts to varying noise sources and users in hearables and rooms.
  • Spatial Audio and Augmented Hearing — multi-microphone sound source localization and tracking in reverberant environments, and spatially selective noise control that preserves target sounds.
  • Real-Time Audio Systems — low-latency, on-device implementation of adaptive and learning-based algorithms on embedded platforms, from prototype to measured performance on physical devices.
