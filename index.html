About

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Nanyang Technological University, supervised by Prof. Woon-Seng Gan. My research is on deep learning-based active noise control (ANC) under dynamic acoustic conditions: noise that moves, changes spectrum, and travels through acoustic paths that vary with the room, the device and the listener.

I am currently an Audio Engineer Intern at Huawei Research Center Singapore, where I implemented feedback ANC on chip for a physical product. I have also worked with the German Aerospace Center (DLR) on aircraft-cabin ANC and with OPPO Research Institute on acoustic positioning.

Research

  • Sensing

    Sound source localization and DoA estimation in reverberant rooms, moving-source tracking, virtual sensing, and direction-preserving ANC.

  • Controlling

    Generative and selective fixed-filter ANC, deep control-filter generation, meta-adaptive filtering, reinforcement learning, and constrained adaptive control.

  • Deploying

    Lightweight, low-latency models on headphones and embedded chips, aircraft-cabin ANC, and distributed multichannel ANC.
